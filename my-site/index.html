Getting there

  • Bus: Lines 3, 5, 6, 72 and 506 to Karolinska Institutet Biomedicum or Hagaplan.
  • Metro / commuter train: St Eriksplan (metro) or Odenplan (pendeltåg), then bus or about 15–20 minutes on foot.
  • Car: Skruven parking garage, Tomtebodavägen 5B. Disabled parking on Nobels väg near the main entrance.
  • Bike: Racks along Nobels väg.
